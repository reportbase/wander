Browsing the stars

A star field without end, browsed with two zooms: the dial for directions, H for distance.

From home every star is a direction, drawn where its readings put it on the wedge. The gold thumb is a rate: press and pan, and the blue hand travels, gently near the centre, hard toward the edge. Let go and the view re-centres on what the hand reached; that stop is the new home. The wheel sets the radius w, how many levels one rung holds. A tap reads a star; it moves nothing.

H lays each ray: near stars in proportion up to H, then six doublings, each the same room. Nearer than H/32 merges into home; past 64H is past the held edge, without end, and not drawn. Change H and no star moves on the screen: H is in no direction. What changes is which stars are held, and their colours.

Keys: arrows push the gold, − = the radius, [ ] H, 0 the start. Other demos: browsing the wedge, the star field in 3D, H is a zoom, all demos.
