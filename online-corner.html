Online, at the corner

An online decision is made now, and a future it cannot see decides what it costs. The offline optimum sees the whole future: it stands in the writer's place. When the decision weighs what it has already spent against what it is about to commit, the worst future charges for the larger of the two, so the worst case depends only on their ratio, is the same at r and 1/r, and is least at the corner, r = 1. When the unknown is a scale, the turn points are levels, and one scale must be supplied: the guarantee is an amount inside it and a ratio past it.

Ski rental

Time runs right, in units of the price. Gold: the rent paid until buying at s, then the price; red: the day the future stops. The dashed line is the writer's corner, T = 1: before it renting is best, after it buying on the first day.

The worst ratio over every future against the levels of s (each tick a doubling). It is 1 + max(s, 1/s), the same at s and 1/s, least at the corner: 2.

Two-ray search

The two rays on the reader's lay of distance: in proportion inside the first leg d₀ (the circle round the start), 1 + ln(x/d₀) past it, so every turn point sits ln b further out, one level a turn. The path runs down the page, leg by leg, until it meets the target.

The ratio for every target on the lay, right (teal) and left (violet). Inside d₀ behind the start it climbs without bound: the extra walking there is exactly 2d₀, an amount. Past d₀ it stays under the dashed constant: a ratio.

The worst ratio against the levels of r, the distance walked before a leg over the leg: 5 + 2(r + 1/r), the same at r and 1/r, 9 at the corner r = 1, b = 2.
