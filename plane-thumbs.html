Two thumbs in the plane

The crosshairs' geometry with one fewer thumb. Two thumbs, square at the eye, the look down their diagonal: whatever is pointed at reads one thumb along each, the corner. One thumb round it is a disc touching both thumbs, at home and at the horizon: from the eye, the whole quarter turn. Tap a thing to point at it.

The writer's plane round the corner, true to scale: the eye, the two thumbs (the square one thumb on a side), the corner (gold), the one-thumb disc touching the thumbs at home and the horizon, the quarter turn's edges.

The reader's view, as the plane above has it: from home (right, along h) through the corner (middle) to the horizon (left, along v): above as a flat screen draws it, below laid by turns, each thing at its angle off the look and as wide as the angle it spans. Near the corner the two rows agree; they part only toward home and the horizon, where the flat screen stretches.
