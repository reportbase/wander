The levels

The circle in the middle holds everything up to the corner, laid in proportion. Outside it, each ring is one doubling (1 to 2, 2 to 4, 4 to 8, …), and each is drawn half as wide as the one before. The rings crowd toward an edge they never reach, and once a ring is narrower than one grain it can no longer be seen.

The reading s = v/h is laid at radius s inside the circle and at 2 − 1/s past it (the unit circle has radius 1), so a doubling past the corner always takes half the room the last one took and the edge, radius 2, is the horizon. Coarsen the grain and the outer levels drop out one per doubling of the grain; how many show is about log₂(radius ÷ grain). Tap or drag on the picture to read a place. Behind it: Serial, Parallel and Nowhere, the level ("2h in place"; the next level nests in the back half). Other demos: the facing, the sweep device, all demos.
