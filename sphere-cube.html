The sphere in the cube

Every point of a turning sphere points to one point of a cube of any size, once its extent is known.

Drag the sphere to turn it, tap it to choose a point; drag around it to look. Other demos: the sphere, the mountain and the thumb, all demos.

h → right · v → up · v₂ → toward you
