The sphere

Three breadths, v, h and f. Each triple is a ray into the wedge between three axes, and the sphere's first octant is one drawing of that wedge. Divide out their scale and two relations are left: an address on the octant. The address points back to the shape, but only as a relation; the scale is lost for good. Change k and nothing on the octant moves.

Breadths and scale

Octant and unit cube

Drag to turn. The octant of the unit sphere, its three corner arcs meeting at v = h = f; the unit cube's three faces at (1, 1, 1). The ray carries the breadths (k times them, the open dot) through the address on the sphere (white) to the face of the largest breadth (square).

The relation set, flat

The octant seen from the corner (each direction divided by v + h + f): its three vertices are the three breadths alone, its centre the corner. Straight lines here are the sphere's great arcs; areas are not to scale.

The shape, and its depth

A body: ball plus relief in three bands (broad, middle, fine), as the bodies in res/. Depth is the relief, r − 1. Its diameter in the reader's pixels sets which bands show (2 to 5 pixels a feature, as bandW on the flying page): far off it is a plain ball. The cross is the address, pointing back to the body.
