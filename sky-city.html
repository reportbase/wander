Sky city

W A S D fly level (shift: eight strides at a time), Space and C up and down, drag or the arrow keys turn, G half way to the thing under the crosshair, B back to twice as far, the wheel sets the thumb, H holds it, T stops and starts time, M the menu.

On a touch screen: drag to look, pinch to set the thumb, hold the arrows to fly, ↑ and ↓ up and down.
