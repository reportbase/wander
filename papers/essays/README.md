# What can be known from where one stands

A series of eighteen essays, read in order, drafted from 28 October 2026. Each opens on something familiar, shows the geometry in
full, says which older work saw which piece, and ends with what it changes. *Reads and Writes* (`../readers-and-writers.md`)
is the source of truth; where an essay and it differ, the essay is the one to fix. Its physics, known physics sorted by
the two tests, is gathered in *Reads and Writes in Physics* (`../reads-and-writes-physics.md`), and fourteen of the essays (all but 8, 10,
12 and 16) end their body with a paragraph, "In physics", on the known physics that carries their geometry. *Observation from Somewhere* and its companions
are archived (`../archive/ofs/`), kept unchanged for reference: their proofs stand, and where their wording differs from the
later rulings, the later rulings stand.

Two ideas run through all of them:
- **Reading and writing.** A reading is a ratio from a standpoint, free and unchanged when the world is scaled. A writing
  states amounts, and costs an amount carried in.
- **The Cauchy.** The heat of a reader's directions, a continuous weight hottest along the look and cooling toward the
  horizon, what its thumbs, pointed at the mountain, give, the circle's geometry
  with no circle held and no second breadth: its median the corner, its far end the horizon, no mean, since directions have
  none and no V was ever in them. Where the essays meet computing, a direction is an address, the heat under it (R707).

Two tests sort any quantity: **scale the world** (reading or writing?) and **change what arrives** (direction or payload, heat or paint?).
Each is an invariance. By the first, reading and writing are the scale-invariant and the scale-variant, the old
distinction of dimensional analysis; the names say more, since they name the act that turns one into the other (an
amount carried in, as no function of invariants can vary) and the vantage that holds each. Together they say why the two
acts never race: a read adds nothing, and a write to the payload moves no direction. The heat is given; the paint is met.
