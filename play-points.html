Far lights

One feature: how a body is drawn once it is smaller than a pixel. Fly through 3,000 bodies. Near ones are discs. Far ones fall under a pixel, and then there is a choice: let them dim by the inverse square, as light does; let them vanish; or keep them a full-bright pixel, the usual shortcut. Compare the skies.

Drag to look around. Bodies are coloured by kind; every surface is equally bright, so a far body that dims keeps its colour and loses only its light.

Behind it: the same signal, a smaller share (the resolution limit, one pixel across) and the labs' LAD run 3. Other sketches: resolving, the navigator's ladder, the sky fills.
