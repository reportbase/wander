Arrays on the sweeps

Lay K places on the quarter turn. A direction from home, read as the ratio v/h, goes to the place k = ⌊K·(2/π)·arctan(v/h)⌋: its share of the turn times K. No length enters. The central sweep meets the same point from the centre of the circle, at twice the angle, and gives it the same place. Four kinds of array lie on those places: the index alone, the values on the index, a constant array, and an array of known length, the writer's.

The chord from home A to B and the circle on it. Gold: the K places from home, the inscribed sweep's equal angles; teal: the same places from the centre, at twice the angle. The lit place is the one the direction falls in, by both sweeps.
