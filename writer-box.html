The writer's box

The writer is a box: one number line on each axis, both signs, every extent an amount. A reader stands inside it: a sphere round one point, and one octant of it, the wedge, its addresses. Each address is a direction, two numbers; it points at a point of the box, three numbers. The one between is the distance, the writer's, never read. Two vantages, the writer's and the reader's; the third view is ranging, a writing.
