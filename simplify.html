One standpoint, simpler

The results below are classical. Each is stated twice: from no standpoint, as the classical statement holds the whole (angles, π, e, limits, totals, every parameter), and from one standpoint (slopes, fractions, one distance). Both are computed at the same values and agree; what changes is what each form needs. Each card ends with where the restatement stops, and what is left over is always a completion, a chart or a unit.

From Reads and Writes §1, "Where restating from one standpoint simplifies, and where it stops" (R1281, R1282). The numbers are classical in every row; the sorting is a reading. Trig and exponential calls are counted as each form runs.
