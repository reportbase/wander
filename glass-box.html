The stained-glass box

The writer holds a box with a picture on every face. A reader stands inside it: a sphere of radius H round one point, and one octant of it, the wedge, its addresses. Light from each pixel travels in along one direction, through the wedge, to the reader, and paints the address it arrives along. The addresses are given; the colours are the world's. No colour says how far it came.
