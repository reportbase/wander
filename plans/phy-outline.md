## What it is, and its rules

- **Builds on *Reads and Writes*** (the single source of truth since R601): its geometry, words and rulings. Where they
  differ, *Reads and Writes* stands and this paper is fixed.
- **Its physics comes from `papers/physics.md`** (the working record) and the plans and runs it names, as the archived
  OFS physics companion's did (R542). A new physics result goes into the record first, then here.
- **No new physics** (R441). Known physics sorted: the form from what a reader can read, the values across a named bridge.
- **Standings**: [exact], [data], [simulation], [classical]. Wander's world is not used, not even as illustration
  (the archived companion kept a [world] standing; dropped here, after the "physics comes first" rule).
- **Failures kept** (§11), as *Reads and Writes* §11 keeps the seeds.
- **Vocabulary**: read/write as acts; heat and paint for the reader, address only where a writer or a machine counts
  (R706, R707); name the thumb wherever H comes up (R618); a reader's far field in ratios (one in two, one in four), a
  writer's counted in thumbs, never "doubling" or "level" in prose (R622, R642); no shape or circle for a reader (R643);
  V never enters a read, never "cancels" from one (R654); π/2, 2/π and the constants the writer's measures (R717).
- **A normal paper**: no ruling numbers or file paths in the body; plans and runs in "Code and data".

## Decisions for Tom

1. **Title.** *Reads and Writes in Physics*, subtitle "Known physics, sorted by what a standpoint can read". The
   archived companion was *What a Reader Recovers*.
2. **Wander's world left out entirely**, simulations kept (they have referees). Or keep the [world] standing as the
   archived companion did.
3. **D6 (Ω_m = 1/π)** in §12 as a registered prediction, its derivation not accepted (as `physics.md` §5.1 has it), or
   left out.
4. **Length.** Aim about 12,000 words, a third of the archived companion, by giving each case one paragraph and its
   numbers and leaving the derivations to the plans.
5. **The order.** Motion and the moving sky first (the cleanest sorts, and TWG new), or light first (the oldest
   calibration case).
