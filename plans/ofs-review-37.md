An audit, pasted by Tom, of OFS, the CS companion and the physics companion under the companion rule (where a companion
differs from OFS, OFS stands and the companion is fixed). It was run on older downloaded copies (`observation-from-somewhere
(7).md`, `observation-from-somewhere-cs (3).md`).

## Made now, in the physics companion

- PH1. §2 "Unit relations": for the reader h and v are both shares of its one length H; a writer takes each as a share of
  its party's breadth (R533). §2 "The two sweeps": the central point ½(h² − v², 2hv) from the chord's middle, (h², hv)
  over the chord of length 1; its height 2hv = sin 2θ in the radius, its foot h² on the chord (OFS §4). The old clause put
  (h², hv), whose height is hv, beside "its height 2hv".
- PH2. "The parallel reader's bell" (§3.3's dictionary row, §8.1), a retired term, is now the central sweep's height
  sin 2θ = sech(ln k) (OFS §4). On the bisector sin γ = sech(ln k) is that height, not a density.
- PH3. §9.3: n_z replaced by ⟨σz⟩ = h² − v² = cos 2θ_i, V = 2hv = sin 2θ_i, θ_i and θ_f named as the pre- and
  post-selection's angles (as `plans/wvr-plan.md` defines them).
- PH4. Aharonov, Albert and Vaidman (1988) cited in §9.3; Bell (1964) and Clauser et al. (1969) in §4.3's Bell row and
  §9.1. Added to the references and cited where the data are used: ESA (1997) for Hipparcos (MAG read the HYG v3.8
  compilation of it), Gaia Collaboration, Smart et al. (2021) for the GCNS, Tully et al. (2023) for Cosmicflows-4,
  Hubble (1929), Henderson (1975).
