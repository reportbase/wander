# 65 formulas containing 2/π (or a simple multiple)

For each: ID, name, field, formula as usually written, and (for C entries) a source.

- **K1. Half-Cauchy distribution function** (Probability): P(|X| ≤ s) = (2/π) arctan s
- **K2. Buffon's needle** (Geometric probability): P(cross) = 2ℓ/(πd), needle length ℓ ≤ line spacing d
- **K3. Viète's formula** (Analysis): 2/π = (√2/2)·(√(2+√2)/2)·(√(2+√(2+√2))/2)·…
- **K4. Arcsine law (Jeffreys's prior for a binomial proportion; Lévy's arcsine law)** (Probability / statistics): P(x ≤ t) = (2/π) arcsin √t
- **K5. Average of a full-wave rectified sine (averaging AC meters)** (Electrical measurement): mean = (2/π)·peak
- **K6. Square pixel (100% fill) MTF at the Nyquist frequency** (Imaging): sinc(½) = sin(π/2)/(π/2) = 2/π
- **K7. Van Vleck relation for one-bit (sign) correlators** (Radio astronomy / signal processing): r_sign = (2/π) arcsin ρ
- **K8. Kosterlitz–Thouless universal jump of the superfluid stiffness** (Condensed matter): K_R(T_c⁻) = 2/π (Nelson–Kosterlitz)
- **K9. Asymptotic relative efficiency of the median vs the mean, and of the sign test vs the t-test, under a normal law** (Statistics): ARE = 2/π
- **K10. Squared mean absolute value of a standard normal; one-bit quantiser loss** (Statistics / communications): (E|Z|)² = 2/π
- **K11. Effective length of a half-wave dipole** (Antennas): ℓ_eff = λ/π = (2/π)(λ/2)
- **K12. Mean speed of a point on the rim of a rolling wheel** (Mechanics): mean speed = 4v/π
- **K13. Particle flux through a line segment in a 2D ideal gas** (Kinetic theory): flux per length = n v̄/π
- **K14. Jordan's inequality** (Analysis): sin x ≥ (2/π)x for 0 ≤ x ≤ π/2
- **K15. Flamant's solution: line load on an elastic half-plane** (Elasticity): σ_r = −(2P/π) cos θ / r
- **K16. Hertz line contact (cylinders)** (Contact mechanics): peak pressure p₀ = 2P/(πa), a the contact half-width
- **K17. Wigner's semicircle law** (Random matrices): density ρ(x) = (2/π)√(1 − x²) on [−1, 1]
- **K18. Sato–Tate distribution** (Number theory): density (2/π) sin²θ on [0, π]
- **K19. Equilibrium measure of an interval (charge on a thin conducting strip; limit density of Chebyshev nodes)** (Potential theory / approximation): density 1/(π√(1 − x²)) on [−1, 1]; on [0, 1], P(x ≤ t) = (2/π) arcsin √t
- **K20. Goemans–Williamson max-cut approximation ratio** (Algorithms): α = (2/π)·min₀<θ≤π θ/(1 − cos θ) ≈ 0.878
- **C1. Wallis product** (Analysis): 2/π = ∏(1 − 1/4n²). Source: Wikipedia, "Wallis product" (Wallis 1656), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wallis_product. " derived by setting x = π/2 in Euler's sine product.)
- **C2. Bauer–Ramanujan series** (Series / number theory): 2/π = Σ(−1)ᵏ(4k+1)[(2k−1)!!/(2k)!!]³. Source: Bauer, "Von den Coefficienten der Reihen von Kugelfunctionen einer Variablen", J. reine angew. Math. 56 (1859) 101–121; it appears in Ramanujan's first letter to Hardy (1913). The form is quoted in Campbell & Levrie (2024), https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/akherim/CampbellLevrie2024.pdf.
- **C3. Square-wave Fourier series** (Fourier analysis): (2c/π)(sin ωx + ⅓ sin 3ωx + …). Source: Wikipedia, "Gibbs phenomenon", square-wave example.  The half-range form b_n = (2/π)∫₀^π f(x) sin nx dx is in e.g. Kreyszig, *Advanced Engineering Mathematics*.
- **C4. Wilbraham–Gibbs overshoot** (Fourier analysis): (2/π)Si(π) = 1.17898. Source: Wikipedia, "Gibbs phenomenon"; Finch, *Mathematical Constants* (CUP 2003) §4.1; Wilbraham (1848).
- **C5. Fourier sine/cosine transforms** (Analysis): F(α) = (2/π)∫₀^∞ f(x) sin αx dx. Source: Wikipedia, "Sine and cosine transforms", citing Coleman, *An Introduction to Partial Differential Equations with MATLAB* (2nd ed., 2013), p. 221.
- **C6. Lebesgue constant, Chebyshev nodes** (Approximation theory): Λₙ ≈ (2/π) log(n+1) + 0.9625. Source: Wikipedia, "Lebesgue constant" (citing Brutman 1997, *Ann. Numer. Math.* 4:111; Rivlin).
- **C7. Kac's real roots** (Probability): Eₙ = (2/π) log n + 0.6257 + …. Source: Edelman & Kostlan, "How many zeros of a random polynomial are real?", Bull. AMS 32 (1995) 1–37, arXiv:math/9501224; Kac, Bull. AMS 49 (1943) 314–320.
- **C8. Alternating permutations** (Combinatorics): Aₙ ~ 2(2/π)^{n+1} n!. Source: Wikipedia, "Alternating permutation"; Stanley, "A survey of alternating permutations", Contemp. Math. 531 (2010) 165–196.
- **C9. Complete elliptic K and the AGM** (Special functions): (2/π)K(k) = 1/agm(1, k′). Source: Wikipedia, "Elliptic integral" and "Arithmetic–geometric mean"; DLMF §19.8. .)
- **C10. Struve H₁ at large argument** (Special functions): H₁(z) → 2/π. Source: Aarts & Janssen, "Approximation of the Struve function H₁ occurring in impedance calculations", J. Acoust. Soc. - **C11. Nesterov's π/2 theorem** (Optimisation / algorithms): (2/π)·SDP ≤ OPT ≤ SDP. Source: Nesterov, "Semidefinite relaxation and nonconvex quadratic optimization", Optim. Methods Softw. 9 (1998) 141–160; CORE DP 1997044, https://ideas.repec.org/p/cor/louvco/1997044.html.
- **C12. Krivine's bound / cut norm** (Functional analysis / algorithms): K_G ≤ π/(2 ln(1+√2)); c = (2/π) ln(1+√2). Source: Wikipedia, "Grothendieck inequality"; Krivine, Adv. Math. 31 (1979) 16–30.
- **C13. Kendall's τ (Greiner)** (Statistics): τ = (2/π) arcsin ρ. - **C16. Pendulum period** (Classical mechanics): T = (2/π)T₀K(sin θ₀/2). Source: Wikipedia, "Pendulum (mechanics)", eq. 3; Cromer, Am. J. Phys. 63 (1995) 112.
