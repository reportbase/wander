# Review 4 of *Reads and Writes*: a fresh referee evaluation of the 12 November draft

12 Nov 2026. SPN R914. A referee report pasted by Tom, with no words of his own, of the draft written in Ω. Scores, the
referee's judgments: originality 9.3, explanatory power 9.0, mathematical correctness 8.3, consistency 8.2, empirical
support 7.7, overall 8.7. Verdict: major revision before publication; continue. By R863 Claude checked each point and
took only what it agrees with.

## What it said, and what was done

1. **The chaos section overstates the local expansion.** §9 said each chart "spreads nearby starts by a factor of two
   a step". **Correct, an error of mine.** Only the tent map does (away from its fold); the logistic map's one-step
   stretch is 4·|1 − 2y|. What the charts share is the Lyapunov rate ln 2, the average of the log-stretch over the
   unchanged law. Fixed. The referee's own remark that the slope chart stretches by two wherever its derivative is
   defined is wrong too: d/ds of 2s/(1 − s²) is 2(1 + s²)/(1 − s²)².
2. **"Read" works at more than one level** (D2, D3, D7). **Agreed.** D2 now keeps three words apart: a reading (one
   direction's slope), a read (all of them from one standpoint at one moment), and reading, the act (continuous,
   unmoved, nothing kept). D3 says two looks held together are reading, two reads and not one.
3. **"A read uses no trigonometry" should stay operation-specific.** **Agreed in part**: the paper already has the
   three grades (rational, square root, limit) in §9; one sentence in §1 now points to them.
4. **The classification is an interpretation, not a physical ontology.** Already the paper's position (marked
   "a reading", D6, O9; R645: no claim about what the world is made of). Nothing changed.
5. **The blueprint's role; proposed sentence "The read supplies the relation; the blueprint supplies its completed
   measurement."** **Not taken as worded.** By R913, ruled the same day, Ω and 1/Ω are the limits reads and writes
   encounter; the blueprint's own is π, both hemispheres at once. The opening paragraph already says no finite sweep
   or count has Ω's value. The referee's care about "belongs" is fair and is met there.
6. **Evidence table; condense repetition later.** Agreed as advice; no edit. A cut is Tom's call (as review 3).

## Not done

The referee's three priorities are items 1–3 above. No structural pass, no new test.
