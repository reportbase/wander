# OFS review 21: ChatGPT on "Two readers, two charts"

*18 October 2026, from Tom.*

ChatGPT agreed that the Greenland and Africa example belongs in OFS. It asked for three adjustments. All three were taken
in the rewritten paragraph after Proposition 5(g)'s four-chart table.

1. **Frame Mercator by the property it keeps.** Mercator keeps local angles, so a constant bearing is a straight line.
   It is not "correct for every address reader".
   - *Made:* the paragraph opens on the angles and constant bearings.
   - *Made:* it adds that "neither reader is thereby obliged to use either: each chart follows from the property its
     reader needs kept".
2. **Keep the globe apart from the writer's representation of it.**
   - *Made:* "Only the globe itself, the complete object, keeps every direction and every area at once; it is the
     common reference from which both charts are drawn". This replaces "only the globe, the complete object the writer
     holds".
3. **Do not let the scale factors carry the argument.**
   - *Made:* the bulleted factors are gone, leaving one illustrative sentence. On the chart Greenland looks about
     two-thirds of Africa's size; Africa is truly about fourteen times larger.
   - The factors themselves were computed from the latitude bands: Greenland is drawn 10 to 12 times its area, Africa
     about 1.15. They are kept in SPN R372.
