## The organizing idea

One scene carries everything: a thing approached, its size and distance unknown. The geometry gives the same reads to
anything that approaches; two paces stand on them, a count that arrives and a ratio that approaches; Euler's −1 is the
arrival. A reader can follow the whole of *Reads and Writes* through this one scene, where the long paper sorts
many.

Working title: *Arriving: one approach, two paces, and Euler's −1*. (Alternatives: *The Walk and the Landing*;
*Walking to the Mountain*.)

## Proposed sections

1. **The scene.** A thumb held out, a mountain in the distance; nothing known but what is seen. What is asked: what is
   given free, what must be kept, what must be carried in.
2. **One look.** A read: a direction, no size, no distance, unmoved by scaling. The corner met by looking ("now the
   mountain is my thumb"). (R&W §1, §3.)
3. **Two looks.** Nothing connects them but something kept. One counted stride between two looks gives the strides left
   and the breadth in strides (WLK W5): a write of the relation, still no unit. (R850, R&W §6.)
4. **The even ladder.** With a steady stride, thumb over mountain falls evenly; the ladder is the writer's line, no
   corner on it; the count from corner to foot is the size. (WLK run 1.)
5. **The other pace.** A fixed share of what remains: one reading held steady, no count, the foot a limit. Even on the
   logarithm. No pace is both. (WLK W7; MOT; Lee.)
6. **Euler's circle.** Z = (1 + is)/(1 − is): far off 1, the corner i, the foot −1. Turn, fixed share and stride as the
   three kinds of map. Reached by a count, approached by a ratio. What is the reader's (rational in s), what is the
   writer's (the angle, π, the completed circle). (WLK run 2; R&W §9.)
7. **Where the unit enters.** The stride in metres; the only place physics comes in. Scale the world and nothing on
   the walk moves. (R851, R852.)
8. **In space, and where it stops.** Two thumbs; the tower example; turns that do not commute. Short.
9. **What is known.** Lee's tau; time to contact; the Cayley transform and the classification of Möbius maps;
   parallax along the line of sight. What is ours: the sorting.
10. **What is not claimed.** Nothing about what any creature does (R261, R262, R455); no new physics.
