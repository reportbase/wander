## Response

The review reads review 14's two edits, the circle's scope and reading and writing as modes, and keeps both. It asks for
two refinements, and both were made in OFS §9's "Reading and writing" on 16 October.

1. **Operation and representation kept apart.** "Writing is the unsituated mode" while a situated observer can write
   ran together the observer's standpoint and the character of what it produces. The paragraph now says that reading
   and writing are operations, not kinds of observer. Reading is the situated operation. Writing produces the
   completed, unsituated representation of situations 1 and 2, stating a thing in amounts. A situated observer can
   write, but only with information its ratios do not contain.
2. **v·V against v·V\*.** The paragraph called "v·V" the act of writing. It now names both products:
   - v·V is the amount in the reader's drawing, which is v·H and writes nothing new;
   - v·V\* is the amount at the other's true breadth, which is the act of writing and needs V\*.

   This keeps the case rule (V the drawn whole, V\* the true breadth) at the moment it matters.
