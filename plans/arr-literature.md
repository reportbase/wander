## What I expect, written before the search

- **A1.** That holding the image's rate of expansion constant gives a distance falling exponentially, touching down at
  near zero speed and in the ideal never arriving: **in print** (honeybee landing; tau theory).
- **A2.** That at constant approach speed the reciprocal of the visual angle (or of its tangent) falls linearly in time
  or distance: **in print** in some form (it is tau = θ/θ̇ restated), though perhaps not as "an even ladder".
- **A3.** The two approach rules classed as the parabolic and hyperbolic maps of one circle or projective line (one
  fixed point against two), with a turn the elliptic: **not found** as a statement about approach.
- **A4.** That under the map s ↦ (1 + is)/(1 − is) the point −1 is the image of the line's far end is **textbook**; that
  the infinity in e^(iπ) = −1 is therefore "the far start of a walk, not a vanishing step": **not found** in those terms.
- **A5.** Ranging by the thumb (the mil relation, "rule of thumb") is **in print**; "the place where a thing covers the
  thumb is found by looking, and strides counted from it give the size": **not found**, or only as fieldcraft.
- **A6.** The corner/size split (what a look gives against what a count gives) as a sorting: **not found**.

If A3, A4 or A6 is found in print, the essay's "what is the series' own" paragraph is wrong and is to be corrected, and
the source cited.
