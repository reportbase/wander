# OFS review 25: recovering is not writing (ChatGPT, 22 October 2026)

*Pasted by Tom after SPN R441 ("physics already is mix of reading and writing observers"). ChatGPT's message, kept as
received in substance, then Claude's response and what was made (SPN R442).*

## ChatGPT's points

- Three distinctions: the writer has the complete structure available as the object described; the situated reader
  meets only what its position and reading relations make available, and can combine observations to recover genuine
  structure without ever possessing the whole; the recovered truth can be objectively correct while remaining partial.
- "A reader can recover truth without recovering the whole truth, and cannot infer completeness merely from the
  correctness of what it has recovered."
- A theory can recover relationships, predict accurately and describe powerfully, yet those achievements alone do not
  say whether it is a complete account of the structure or a successful account of what a situated observer can read.
- Nuance: the writer's completeness is a role in the framework, not something a situated reader may assume it has.
- Summary: "The situated reader may recover truth, but recovery does not make the reader the writer. What has been
  recovered may be exact and still be partial; its completeness cannot be established merely by the success of the
  reading."

## Response (Claude)

- Already in the corpus: no reading gives V*/H (OFS Proposition 8); writing needs an amount carried in (R293–R297);
  a tree of readings cannot find its own error and a second route can ("Closing a loop", R321); the writer is a vantage
  held by walking the whole or carrying in its measure, not a view from nowhere (R335, R300).
- Corrected: "the writer has the complete structure available" reads as a view from nowhere; a writer's whole reaches
  only as far as it walked or carried in. EBD's own comparison was between two observers: the CMB's β is itself a
  writing (a Doppler frequency read against a rest frequency).
- New, taken: correctness does not establish completeness; completeness is never a reading, since a reader can show
  only incompleteness, by a disagreeing route. An instance: EBD's four flux slices agreed on one β (a consistent
  recovery), and only the colour slices, a second route, showed part of the dipole is no boost.
- Not taken into OFS: any claim about whether a physical theory is complete (OFS judges no other work, R364).
