## Response

All three taken; no mathematics changed.

1. The abstract, the Results bullet, §5's "The order of the chain" and "Why the circle", and §7.1's "Working back from
   the Cauchy" now say the half-Cauchy on one quadrant, its median at the corner and its quartiles a reciprocal pair, and
   the standard Cauchy signed over two quadrants. The CS companion likewise (§1's "One length", §4).
2. "The order of the chain" names each ingredient: H the one length, the turn the circle, the chart s = v/h the reading,
   the uniform measure the law; that the reader holds H alone means only that no second length enters.
3. The same paragraph closes: the family V = cH describes other external drawings, none of it a choice the reader makes
   to set up its own geometry (already the sense of "Where c ≠ 1 comes from").
