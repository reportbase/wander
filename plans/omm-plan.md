# OMM: the matter fraction from the reader's geometry

*Plan written 10 October 2026, before any derivation or data is worked (Claude; Tom: "yes" to taking D6 forward in the
current terms). D6, "Dark Energy and Matter Fractions; Redshift Predictions" (Tom's status note of 23 September 2026,
uploaded 10 October; not in this repo), derived Ω_m = ψ₁ψ₂ = 1/π from the geometry of its day. Tom, 10 October: it "was
set aside because we had gone as far we could at the time. we have progressed far since that paper was written." This
plan asks whether today's geometry carries it further. The lab rules hold: predictions and kills are written here first,
and nothing above the "Runs" line is edited after a stage is worked.*

## What D6 claimed, and what it rested on

- **Assumed:** the Friedmann model of an expanding universe, taken as a special case (D16/D17). Expansion is settled
  physics; the plan does not question it. It is a bridge physics supplies (SPN Part II).
- **Derived:** Ω_m = ψ₁ψ₂ = 1/π ≈ 0.3183; Ω_Λ = 1 − 1/π; then by ordinary cosmology (matter as (1+z)³, w = −1)
  z_eq = (π − 1)^⅓ − 1 ≈ 0.289 and z_acc = (2(π − 1))^⅓ − 1 ≈ 0.624 (arithmetic checked 10 October).
- **Rested on:** ψ₁ = 2/π and "the inside observer reads ψ₁ψ₂ of the sphere per observation", from the archived ψ₁ paper,
  which is not to be cited. Matter was "chord content, at one point", dark energy "the arc residual, everywhere".
- **Built in, not predicted:** Ω_total = 1, since Ω_Λ is defined as 1 − Ω_m.

This plan uses none of the archived paper. Every step is restated in SPN's current terms: fill levels h and v on [0, 1],
the sweep, the facing, the one corner (R188), V = H creating the unit circle (R188, R189), the levels as plain
logarithmic doublings (R189), addresses and payload (§4.3), and the lay (`flight.html`).

## Keep the case

- **Geometry** (fill levels, shares, the sweep, the facing) has no scale. Ω_m is a share, a ratio of two densities of
  the same kind, so it can be geometry: no bridge is needed for the number itself.
- **Physics** supplies everything with an amount: the expansion history, H₀, how matter dilutes with z, and every
  measured Ω. z_eq and z_acc need the bridge (Friedmann's equations); they are physics applied to a geometric share.

## Stage 1: the share, in today's terms (exact arithmetic; rulings needed)

The question: what share of the whole does one reading hold, and is it 1/π?

- **S1a (ψ₁).** One sweep is the bar going from all of h to all of v, stepped evenly in its turn, from home. The share of
  the bar that falls on h, averaged over that sweep, is 2/π exactly. *Prediction:* this is the quantity D6 called ψ₁.
  *Ruling needed:* is stepping evenly in the turn the right weight for "per observation"? (SPN §14 says the weight on the
  sweep is a choice.) If another weight is ruled, the average is recomputed and that number replaces 2/π here.
- **S1b (ψ₂).** One observation holds one facing of the two the reader has. *Prediction:* that halving is D6's ψ₂ = ½.
  *Ruling needed:* is one facing per observation the right reading?
- **S1c (the product).** If both stand, the share is (2/π)·(½) = 1/π.
- **Kill (stage 1):** the share one reading holds, worked in current terms with the weights ruled, is not 1/π. Then D6's
  number does not follow from today's geometry, and that is recorded as the result; stage 3 is not run.

## Stage 2: what matter and dark energy are on the lay (rulings needed, before any data)

- **Candidate (Claude's reading, 10 October; not ruled):** the arc is the addresses, laid everywhere and the same in
  every direction (isotropic, unclustered: D6's dark energy); the chord is the payload, met at one place (localised,
  clustering: D6's matter). On the lay, the addresses fill the whole disc; the payload sits at points on it.
- *Ruling needed:* does that reading stand? If it does, condition (c) below (dark energy clusters) is a direct test of it:
  addresses cannot cluster.
- **Worked both ways instead of ruled** (Tom, 10 October: "lets not rule, but try it both ways"). *Added before stage 2
  was worked.* Two readings: **X**, the candidate above (matter the payload, dark energy the addresses), and **Y**, its
  swap (matter the addresses, dark energy the payload). Each is held against what physics measures of the two, stated
  here first, as settled behaviour, not numbers:
  - **B1, clustering.** Matter clusters (galaxies, clusters, the cosmic web); dark energy shows no clustering.
  - **B2, dilution.** Matter's amount per volume falls as the universe expands, as (1+z)³ back in time; dark energy's
    stays constant (w = −1 within current errors).
  - **B3, the same everywhere.** Dark energy is the same in every direction; matter is not.
  - **B4, the share today.** Matter is the smaller share now, about a third; dark energy the larger.
  - **B5, the share changes with epoch.** Measured back in time, matter's share grows (it dominated before z ≈ 0.3).
  *Kill (a reading):* it contradicts any of B1–B3 outright. B4 and B5 are reported, since they bear on stage 3.

## Stage 3: the comparison with data (only after stages 1 and 2 stand)

**Data.** Published values, transcribed with their sources, each re-verified against its paper before the comparison and
recorded below the "Runs" line with the date: Planck 2018 (PR3, TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing; and PR4 where it differs) and
DESI DR2 BAO alone. As recalled when this plan was written, to be checked: Planck Ω_m = 0.315 ± 0.007; DESI DR2 BAO alone
Ω_m = 0.2975 ± 0.0086. The astronomy archives are blocked from this environment; the papers on arXiv are the source.

**Predictions** (Ω_m = 1/π = 0.31831):
- **P1 (Planck).** 1/π lies within 2σ of Planck's Ω_m. *Killed* if outside 2σ.
- **P2 (DESI DR2 BAO alone).** 1/π lies within 3σ of DESI's Ω_m. *Killed* if outside 3σ. (As recalled it sits about 2.4σ
  away, which D6 called "1–2σ"; the stricter figure is used here.)
- **P3 (the two together).** If Planck and DESI disagree with each other by more than 2σ, the comparison reports both and
  claims neither; agreement with one alone is not counted as support.
- z_eq and z_acc are not separate tests: they follow from Ω_m and w by the Friedmann bridge, so they stand or fall with it.

**D6's own kill conditions, kept:** (a) Ω_m evolving with epoch in a way l = 2 back-reaction cannot account for; (b) w(z)
evolution the l = 2 calculation, once done, cannot account for; (c) any detection that dark energy clusters.

## What would not be shown

- **That w = −1.** D6's w = −1 is a symmetry argument at the monopole; the observed pull toward evolving dark energy
  (2.8–4.2σ in D6's table) waits on the l = 2 back-reaction (D6's open item M2), which this plan does not attempt.
- **Flatness.** Ω_total = 1 is built in.
- **The expansion.** Taken from physics, not derived.
- **That a close number means a cause.** 1/π near 0.315 is one number against one measurement; stages 1 and 2 have to
  stand on their own first, which is why they are ruled before the data is opened.
