## What D6 claimed, and what it rested on

- **Assumed:** the Friedmann model of an expanding universe, taken as a special case (D16/D17). Expansion is settled
  physics; the plan does not question it. It is a bridge physics supplies (SPN Part II).
- **Derived:** Ω_m = ψ₁ψ₂ = 1/π ≈ 0.3183; Ω_Λ = 1 − 1/π; then by ordinary cosmology (matter as (1+z)³, w = −1)
  z_eq = (π − 1)^⅓ − 1 ≈ 0.289 and z_acc = (2(π − 1))^⅓ − 1 ≈ 0.624 (arithmetic checked 10 October).
- **Rested on:** ψ₁ = 2/π and "the inside observer reads ψ₁ψ₂ of the sphere per observation", from the archived ψ₁ paper,
  which is not to be cited. Matter was "chord content, at one point", dark energy "the arc residual, everywhere".
- **Built in, not predicted:** Ω_total = 1, since Ω_Λ is defined as 1 − Ω_m.

This plan uses none of the archived paper. Every step is restated in SPN's current terms: fill levels h and v on [0, 1],
the sweep, the facing, the one corner (R188), V = H creating the unit circle (R188, R189), the levels as plain
logarithmic doublings (R189), addresses and payload (§4.3), and the lay (`flight.html`).

## Keep the case

- **Geometry** (fill levels, shares, the sweep, the facing) has no scale. Ω_m is a share, a ratio of two densities of
  the same kind, so it can be geometry: no bridge is needed for the number itself.
- **Physics** supplies everything with an amount: the expansion history, H₀, how matter dilutes with z, and every
  measured Ω. z_eq and z_acc need the bridge (Friedmann's equations); they are physics applied to a geometric share.

## Stage 1: the share, in today's terms (exact arithmetic; rulings needed)

The question: what share of the whole does one reading hold, and is it 1/π?

- **S1a (ψ₁).** One sweep is the bar going from all of h to all of v, stepped evenly in its turn, from home. The share of
  the bar that falls on h, averaged over that sweep, is 2/π exactly. *Prediction:* this is the quantity D6 called ψ₁.
  *Ruling needed:* is stepping evenly in the turn the right weight for "per observation"? (SPN §14 says the weight on the
  sweep is a choice.) If another weight is ruled, the average is recomputed and that number replaces 2/π here.
- **S1b (ψ₂).** One observation holds one facing of the two the reader has. *Prediction:* that halving is D6's ψ₂ = ½.
  *Ruling needed:* is one facing per observation the right reading?
- **S1c (the product).** If both stand, the share is (2/π)·(½) = 1/π.
- **Kill (stage 1):** the share one reading holds, worked in current terms with the weights ruled, is not 1/π. Then D6's
  number does not follow from today's geometry, and that is recorded as the result; stage 3 is not run.

**Predictions** (Ω_m = 1/π = 0.31831):

**D6's own kill conditions, kept:** (a) Ω_m evolving with epoch in a way l = 2 back-reaction cannot account for; (b) w(z)
