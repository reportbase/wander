## ChatGPT's points

- 7.8/10, major revision before submission, "not a restart of the mathematics". Scores: correctness of the central
  calculations 8.5, internal consistency 8, logical rigor 7, originality of the synthesis 8, central thesis 8, higher
  dimensions 7.5, applications 7.5, exposition 5.5.
- Established: the ratio separates scale from direction ((vV*)/(hH) = (v/h)(V*/H)); the half-Cauchy from a uniform
  quarter turn, exactly; the median, the missing mean and the exact Cauchy kept as three results with different
  premises; the assumptions increasingly explicit.
- The address/payload separation in three claims: as mathematics (a domain apart from values) established; as this
  situated construction established conditionally; as "what geometry is for every situated reader" an interpretive
  thesis. A physical observer's operationally available addresses can change with the signal (occlusion, resolution).
- The main pressure point: what forces V = H. The construction (a rigid Euclidean turn with equal breadths gives the
  Cauchy) and the uniqueness (wanting that turn and its standard law selects c = 1) are defensible; necessity for every
  reader does not follow.
- Claims audited: the logarithmic far field "the only consistent one" is conditional on (S) and the regularity
  conditions behind it; a fixed address capacity "holds any N" only in a limited sense (it cannot be lossless; payloads
  collapse to one address); "a mean asks addresses for sizes they do not carry" an interpretation (other charts, ξ = ln
  v/h, have moments).
- Higher dimensions sound where classical (orthant, ratio coordinates, spherical normalization, Cauchy-type laws); the
  general claims to be qualified. The applications illustrate, they do not validate.
- Seven objections a referee could raise: the Cauchy is classical; the uniform turn chosen and its law obtained; the
  separation is just domain and value (the most important novelty defense, "one compact paragraph early in the
  introduction"); the geometry stipulated; physical observers lack addresses before a signal; the logarithm another
  choice; the applications not independent.
- Asks: a claim ledger; the analogies moved to an appendix; a one-page core construction; V = H stated as conditional
  uniqueness; the novelty paragraph; the log's consistency conditions; finite capacity not lossless; universal language
  scoped.
