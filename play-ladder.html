The navigator's ladder

One feature: distances you earn. Ten bodies of two kinds, at distances you are not told. Start with parallax: it works only for the near ones. Log a distance and you learn that kind's size, which opens width for its kind, out to where it is under a pixel; and its light, which opens light, which reads at any distance, though fewer photons arrive the farther you look.

Each tool use is a turn. A reading shows its own error bar. "Log" keeps it as your distance and reveals whether it was within 10%. Logging a body teaches its kind's size and light, from your logged distance: log a bad one and the error is carried up the ladder.

Behind it: the ladder and the labs' LAD (the distance ladder). Other sketches: far lights, resolving, the sky fills.
