Resolving

One feature: the moments a body changes as you come in. It is the same planet all the way; what you catch of it grows with its size in pixels. A dimming point; then one pixel across, a disc (now you can read its size); then its shape, its terrain, its surface. Each first crossing is an event, the kind a game can reward.

Bands fade in over a factor of 2.5 in size, as the flying page's do (bandW), so nothing pops. Under one pixel the point's brightness is its area in pixels: the inverse square.

Behind it: the same signal, a smaller share (the resolution limit, one pixel across) and Serial, Parallel and Nowhere §3.3 ("Smaller drives less detailed"). Other sketches: far lights, the navigator's ladder, the sky fills.
