Three thumbs, walking

W A S D walk (shift: eight strides at a time), G half way to the thing under the crosshair, B back to twice as far, drag or the arrow keys turn, the wheel sets the thumb, H holds it, M the menu. T stops and starts time: the boxes in the sky move, each scale at its own velocity, found where they are now by scanning the cells about the eye in their own frame (nothing stepped or stored).

On a touch screen: drag with one finger to look, pinch to set the thumb (fingers apart, a larger thumb), hold ▲ ◀ ▼ ▶ to walk, ×8 for eight strides at a time, G, B and H as the keys.
