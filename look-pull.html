A look and a pull

One place, and matter about it. A look from the place faces one way and keeps its directions apart. A pull at the place has no facing: every direction is taken in at once, each with its amount. Give the pull a facing for a moment and the two can be set side by side. A look's two halves add to everything there is. A pull's two halves subtract, and what is left is the pull along the facing.
