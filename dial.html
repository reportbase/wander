The H dial

A reader with no model of what it sees, only its own pixels, H wide. The system is two points, one apart. s = 1/H is the system against the reader's grain. Turn the dial and look: below one pixel across, "two" comes only by chance; past it, every time. The cheapest setting is one pixel across.

Chance of "two" in one look

Line: min(s, 1). Dots: what your looks gave, by s. Below one pixel across it is a chance, s; from one pixel across on it is certain.

What a sure "two" costs

One unit for each pixel the system spans, on each look, until "two". Line: (1 + s)·max(1, 1/s), least at one pixel across and the same at s and 1/s. Dots: measured means.

From plans/grn-plan.md, runs 3 to 5 (synthetic, each predicted before it ran). Runs 1 and 2 gave the reader the shape ("two equal stars"); such a reader reads below its grain, and the resolution limit blurs. This page is the reader without a model. See also the sweep device.
