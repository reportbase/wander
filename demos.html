Wander demos

Wander is a world of solids you fly through, and a lab that compares what a reader can recover from arriving signals against known physics. These are its pages: the world, the lab, and three small demos of one idea each.

the world

Fly

The world itself: suns, planets, moons and belts, drawn only from the signals that reach you, with a readout of what can be recovered from them. Hold W to gather speed and let go to glide; tap beside a planet to go round it, scroll to come in close. Far off a body is a plain ball; close up its craters and mountains show.

Try: flywithout the lab button

the lab

The lab

Every experiment, one card each. Each lab writes down its prediction and what would kill it before it runs, and keeps the result, killed or not. Run one from its card, or all of them at once and read the full report.

Try: the cardsrun every labrun BAL

a demo: the sweep

The sweep device

Pairs (v, h), each divided by its own h, laid on the unit sweep g = (2/π)·atan(v/h): from 0 at home through ½ at the corner toward 1 at the horizon. A cursor reads them one at a time (serial) or all at once (parallel), with the quarter circle, divided by the whole, beside it.

Try: planetsa shapea spreadthe number lineparallel

a demo: the reader's grain

The h dial

A reader with no model, only its own pixels h wide, looks at two points one apart. Below its corner it sees "two" only by chance; from the corner on, every time. Turn the dial, look, slide h, or approach from afar. The cheapest sure reading is at the corner, and costs the same at s and 1/s.

Try: below the cornerat the cornerpast it

a demo: three breadths

The sphere

Three breadths (v, h, f) and a scale. Divide out the scale and an address on the sphere's first octant is left; the scale is lost for good. The octant splits into six chambers that meet at the corner v = h = f; a reader holding h can be near in one direction and far in another. Beside it, a body whose relief (its depth) shows only once it covers enough of the reader's pixels.

Try: the cornerfar in botha held wholefar offclose up
