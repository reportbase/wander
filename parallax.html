Parallax from the corner

Two standpoints, a baseline B apart, each read only directions. Alone, each holds a figure of rays: every direction, no distance, at any scale. Together, with the baseline's length, the rays cross and the figure comes back with its size. Parallax has a corner of its own: the circle with the baseline as its diameter, where the baseline subtends a right angle. There its error is least, and round. With every level away the error doubles and stretches, along the line of sight outside the circle and along the baseline inside, until, past a band set by the step, a point is a ray again: what one standpoint holds alone. Parallax needs situated observation, and returns to it.

The plane, laid as the reader lays it

By direction from the baseline's middle, and by distance in half-baselines, in proportion inside the corner circle and one equal step per doubling past it (H set to half the baseline). The corner circle is parallax's corner: the baseline seen at a right angle. Each target is triangulated thirty times with directions off by the step: teal held to within 5%, gold stretched, red lost to a ray. White rings: the targets' true places.

The bell: parallax's precision across levels

Across the levels of distance from the corner (half the baseline). Gold: the relative error in distance, ε·cosh of the levels, least at the corner and doubling with each level away on either side; it is the parallel reader's bell, upside down. Violet: how stretched the error is, along the line of sight over along the baseline, round (0) at the corner. Shaded: the band where parallax gives a distance at all.

Keep the case: B, d and the step are amounts; each standpoint holds only directions, which have no scale. The pair (half-baseline, distance) is named, and its corner is the Thales circle; it is not the reader's own corner against anything, though laying the plane with H at the half-baseline puts the two together. The exact results are PXC: the relative error per unit angle cosh(ln k) to 50 digits, the error round at the corner and stretched by d/(B/2) elsewhere, the band acosh(1/ε)/ln 2 levels each side.
