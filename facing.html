The facing

One sweep from A to B. The straight line holds only where you are between them; the reader also faces the other, and that facing is a second degree of freedom. At A you face B; at B you face A; at the corner you face square to the line, equally between them. That is why the corner is at 45°, not 0° or 90°.

The line from A to B, and the facing (the arrow): toward B, square, toward A.

The quarter arc of (h, v) beside the straight line h + v = 1: the arc stands off the line most at the corner.

Two angles, one sweep: the sweep's θ runs a quarter turn, the facing a half turn, facing = 2θ (the inscribed angle, Thales). Geometry only, in fill levels: no breadth and no distance enters. Behind it: Serial, Parallel and Nowhere §1, "The line, the arc and the facing". Other demos: the sweep device, the sphere, all demos.
