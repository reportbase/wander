Sweeping from zero

You cannot go to a spot on the curve; you can only turn on from where you are. Where you start is zero. Turning, you pass every reading v/h once, in order, and the share of the sweep already passed is the turn so far: the turn measured by itself cannot be uneven. Counted by its own turning, what it passes is the Cauchy, half of it below the corner. Count the same turn against a clock, a screen or what arrives, and the count changes, not the turn. Stop, and where you stopped is the new zero.

Home at the middle; zero to the right, the hand turned through θ (gold). Each tick on the quarter is one count of the readings (teal once passed); where they bunch, the count is denser per degree. Faint rays: the earlier zeros, behind.
