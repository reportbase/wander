Walking the room

A reader walks round a room and, wherever it stands, does both sweeps. The inscribed sweep, its hand turned once round, receives directions only: where each thing is from here, the layout. The central sweep runs from each thing's own centre: where the reader stands gives the direction, and what arrives is the radius along it, an amount in a unit nothing fixes; walking round a thing collects its shape. The readout keeps the situation: what has been read, what is held, what is written, and what is still unknown. Through the window a tree: move it away and the walk can no longer go round it, so its shape is lost and its place comes from parallax alone; further still, it is only a direction.

The floor. Tap to walk there. Gold: the inscribed sweep's directions from where the reader stands. Teal: the central sweep's radii, from each thing's centre toward every standpoint taken.
