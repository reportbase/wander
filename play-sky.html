The sky fills

One feature: you see a star only once its light reaches you. If the world starts when you do, the sky starts dark and fills as you play, nearest first, a shell at a time. The count of stars you see is a clock: it reads how long you have been here. Compare "instant light", the usual game, where the whole sky is there at once.

The whole sky, longitude across, latitude up. Each star's brightness is its light thinned by 1/d²: the near ones arrive first and brightest; the far ones last and faintest.

Behind it: the dark sky and the labs' OLB (the dark sky): the dark reads how long the bodies have been sending. Other sketches: far lights, resolving, the navigator's ladder.
