The central reader

The fingers are free; the light draws on them. Wherever the reader looks, its fingers are there: the uniform turn's directions, the Cauchy, laid by looking. It sends nothing, turns through nothing and computes nothing. The payload is the light the world sends along each finger; to read it is to read what the light drew on the fingers, from the same standpoint (step 1). The steps after it are the classical pieces of reading a shape from its centre, shown as before.
