The chain from H

One value starts everything. Pick an H and every whole is drawn at it; the count of other parties sets the shape; one observation is given only the Cauchy fisheye of one facing; the shape, and any figure in it, is built over many observations from standpoints a baseline apart, which is parallax; and only then does physics assign the values.
