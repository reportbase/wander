The sweep device

Any array of pairs (v, h), in any units, divided by its own h: every pair becomes a relation v/h and an address on the one unit sweep, g from 0 (home) through 1/2 (the corner, v = h) toward 1 (the horizon). A cursor then reads the array as a device would: serially, one address at a time, or in parallel, all at once.

One pair per line: label, v, h (or v h). Nothing else is assumed: no units, no scale.

The unit sweep is the number line laid on the unit line; the quarter circle is the same array divided by the whole instead (SPN §2.1: "The number line laid twice", "Two normalizations"). Front of the corner, v is counted in h's; past it, h is counted in v's, and each doubling past the corner is one dyadic level.
