One blueprint in 3D

The same property in three dimensions. The writer has the model, every length in metres, heights too. The payload reader stands in a room and receives a range along each of 2,048 directions, in a unit it never reads: a shell, the room's shape and not its size. The address reader stands in a floor corner, the room one wedge, and receives directions alone, [h : v : v₂]. Scale the world and only the writer's numbers change. Write the room: one counted stride turns the address reader's two looks into metres. The plan in 2D.

The model, in metres

Every length an amount, stated whole: each room's width, depth and height, the lot's extent. The metres need a unit measured beforehand: the scale bar. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a floor to stand the payload reader there (teal); the address reader is at the gold corner, its three walls h, v (up) and v₂.

A shell in an unread unit

2,048 given directions; along each a range arrives, drawn over the ranges' geometric mean (the dashed sphere, its size never read), each dot the colour of what it met. Farther than 2.2 of the mean is drawn at 2.2; open sky, faint at the rim. Scale the world and nothing here moves.

From home: the triangle

Looking out of the corner, the wedge is the triangle h + v + v₂ = 1, every address at its shares (h bottom left, v at the top, v₂ bottom right). The dashed medians are the three pairs' corners: v = h gold, v₂ = h white, v₂ = v violet. The near field, v and v₂ both at most h, is the part outlined in teal by home; it holds a third. The dots never move; their colours are the payload.

Lambert's map of the wedge

Azimuth across (h on the left, v₂ on the right), height v up: the 64 × 64 addresses even, one cell each, painted by what each met. The corners dashed: v = h (gold), v₂ = h (the middle line), v₂ = v. Half the cells either side of each, in every room.

The writer is the vantage that writes: it needs the whole and a unit. The two readers are what one turn of the head gives: the payload reader keeps the amount along each direction, the address reader the direction itself. One stride counted is the amount that turns the address reader's looks into the writer's metres. Other demos: the blueprint, the room in ratios, the wedge, all demos.
